About

Max Boganets is a Russian-born writer living in Illinois. He writes literary and speculative fiction exploring technology, society, and human identity.

The author of eleven peer-reviewed scientific papers, he enjoyed writing short fiction at school and has since returned to it as a hobby. His stories explore what happens when discoveries are misunderstood, familiar technologies conceal unsettling truths, or apparently reasonable decisions carry an unexpected human cost. He also writes about grief, connection, and finding the courage to move forward.

Research and revision are central to his process. He works through the logic of a world, its characters, and its mechanisms before cutting back explanations and reshaping the story—even when that means discarding passages he likes.

Away from writing, he designs and scratch-builds scale models, from prototype and drawings to construction and paint. His work has won science-fiction category awards at IPMS shows and a silver medal at the 51st MMSI show.
